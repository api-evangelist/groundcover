Dashboards actually allow agents to say more and they’re making dashboards more important than ever. A link from @groundcover into a dashboard that summarizes the investigation you performed with the Agent is the fastest way to communicate between teammates around the problem-scape. The conversation around it spawns additional Agent requests like building monitors and alerts on top of those very same dashboards.
